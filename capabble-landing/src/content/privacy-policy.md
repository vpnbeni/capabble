## Who we are

[Capabble legal entity name], of [registered address] ("Capabble", "we"), makes school management software used by principals, office staff, teachers, parents and students.

This policy follows India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. For the information a school puts into Capabble, **the school is the data fiduciary** and we process it on the school's behalf and on its instructions. For your workspace account and your visits to our website, Capabble is the data fiduciary.

## What we collect

### When a school creates a workspace account

- Institution code, affiliation no. and institution name.
- The email address used to sign in. Passwords are stored only in a form that can't be read back.

### What schools add while using Capabble

- Students and parents: names, class and section, admission no., contact details, attendance, marks, fees and, where the Clinic module is used, health notes.
- Staff: names, roles, contact details, timetables and duties.
- Transport, assets and activities records, if those modules are turned on.

### When anyone uses Capabble

- Device and browser type, IP address, sign-in times and error logs, so we can keep accounts secure and fix problems.

## Children's data

Most student records are about children. We process them only to provide the service to the school, and we **never** use them to track, profile or advertise to children, or sell them to anyone.

Schools are responsible for getting verifiable consent from a parent or guardian where the law requires it, before adding a child's data.

## How we use it

- To run the modules your school has turned on.
- To keep accounts secure, for example by spotting unusual sign-ins.
- To send service emails such as account confirmations and password reset links.
- To answer support requests.
- To improve Capabble, using only combined data that doesn't identify anyone.
- To meet our legal obligations.

## Who we share it with

We share personal data only with service providers who help us run Capabble, under contracts that limit their use of it to that work:

- Hosting: Vercel (website) and Amazon Web Services, Mumbai region (application and data)
- Database: MongoDB Atlas (school data) and Neon (billing records)
- File storage: Cloudinary (photos and documents uploaded to Capabble)
- Payments: Razorpay (card, UPI and bank payments for fees you pay us)
- Email delivery: Hostinger (emails we send from no-reply@capabble.cloud)

We disclose data to authorities only when the law requires it. We do not sell personal data.

## Where it's kept, and for how long

Data is stored in India, in the Amazon Web Services Mumbai region.

- Workspace account details: while the account is open, and for 90 days after it's closed.
- School data: kept for 30 days after the workspace closes, so the school can export it, including past report cards. We then delete it within a further 60 days, or hand it back first if the school asks.
- Backups: overwritten within 30 days.
- Invoices and payment records: kept for as long as Indian tax law requires, currently 8 years.

If a school wants records such as report cards to stay available to past students, it should export them before its workspace closes.

## How we protect it

- Data is encrypted while it travels between your device and Capabble, and while stored by our database providers.
- Passwords are stored only in a form that can't be read back.
- People see only what their role in the school allows.
- Our staff's access to school data is limited and logged.

If a breach affects your data, we'll tell the school and the Data Protection Board of India as the law requires.

## Your rights

You can ask to see the personal data we hold about you, correct it, have it erased, and nominate someone to act for you. You can also raise a grievance.

For data your school added, such as a student's record, ask your school first. They control that data, and we'll help them respond.
