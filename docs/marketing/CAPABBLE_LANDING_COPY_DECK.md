## 2) Problem Statement (Operational Exam Chaos)
Exam operations are often split across paper registers, spreadsheets, WhatsApp updates, and last-minute manual coordination. This creates avoidable delays and high-risk points around seating allocation, subject-day planning, and answer-sheet accountability.

Capabble addresses these operational gaps by giving examination teams one structured system to import official data, organize centre-specific plans, and execute exam-day tasks with less confusion.

### Operational Pain Points This Page Should Call Out
- Fragmented records across multiple files and formats.
- Delayed coordination between timetable, rooms, and functionaries.
- Seating mistakes caused by disconnected candidate and subject data.
- Weak tracking of received, used, discarded, and balance answer sheets.
- Compliance stress when guidelines, circulars, and formats are not centrally available.

---

## 3) Solution Overview (One Unified Exam Operations System)
Capabble is a unified examination operations platform for schools and exam centres. It combines data import, centre-specific planning, seating outputs, answer-sheet lifecycle tracking, and daily duty management in one connected workflow.

### What You Can Run From One Dashboard
- Import and manage CBSE datesheet data.
- Import and maintain candidate records.
- Generate centre-specific datesheets.
- Process Form 66 records by date and subject.
- Generate printable seating plan PDFs in multiple official formats.
- Track answer-sheet inventory across received, used, discarded, and balance states.
- Assign daily duties to exam functionaries by room.
- Access guidelines, circulars, and key CBSE portals from the same system.
